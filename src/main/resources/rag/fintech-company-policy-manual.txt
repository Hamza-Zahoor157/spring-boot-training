1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
====================================================================

RedPay Financial Services is a fictional digital financial institution
created for educational purposes. The company provides digital wallets,
domestic money transfers, international remittance services, merchant
payments, utility bill payments, QR code payments, virtual cards and
financial APIs for third-party partners.

The company operates under strict regulatory compliance requirements.
Every employee is responsible for protecting customer information,
maintaining financial integrity and ensuring that every transaction
performed through the platform complies with applicable laws and
internal policies.

The organization follows a security-first architecture. Every software
system is developed using secure software development lifecycle
principles. Every deployment undergoes automated testing, static code
analysis, dependency vulnerability scanning and security review before
being released into production.

The company stores customer information securely using encryption both
during transmission and at rest. Personally identifiable information
is only accessible to employees whose job responsibilities require such
access.

Every employee must complete mandatory security awareness training
before receiving production system access. Annual refresher training
is required to maintain compliance.

Customer trust is considered the company's most valuable asset.
Business decisions should always prioritize customer security,
confidentiality and regulatory compliance over rapid feature delivery.

====================================================================
2. VISION AND MISSION
====================================================================

The vision of RedPay Financial Services is to provide secure,
accessible and reliable digital financial services while maintaining
the highest standards of regulatory compliance and customer trust.

The mission of the company is to simplify digital financial services
without compromising security or privacy.

Every engineering decision should satisfy three primary principles.

First, security should always be considered before convenience.

Second, customer information must remain confidential throughout its
entire lifecycle.

Third, every financial transaction must be traceable through secure
audit logs.

Software engineers are encouraged to design systems that remain
available during failures, recover quickly after incidents and support
continuous improvement through monitoring and observability.

Operational excellence is considered equally important as feature
development.

3. CUSTOMER ONBOARDING POLICY
====================================================================

Every customer must complete the onboarding process before accessing
financial services.

Customer onboarding begins with account registration through the
official mobile application or web portal.

During registration the customer provides the following information.

• Full legal name
• National identity number
• Mobile phone number
• Email address
• Residential address
• Date of birth
• Nationality

Customers must create a strong password according to the company's
password policy.

The onboarding system automatically validates the email address using
a verification link.

The registered mobile number must be verified through a one-time
password sent via SMS.

Accounts remain in a restricted state until both the email address and
mobile phone number have been verified successfully.

The onboarding workflow records timestamps for every verification step.

Every onboarding request receives a unique onboarding identifier which
is retained for audit purposes.

Customer onboarding information is encrypted before being stored in the
database.

Temporary onboarding data is automatically deleted after thirty days if
registration is not completed.

Incomplete onboarding sessions are periodically removed by automated
maintenance jobs.

Repeated failed onboarding attempts originating from the same device
may trigger temporary rate limiting.

The onboarding service continuously evaluates suspicious behaviour such
as multiple registrations using identical identification numbers or
device fingerprints.

4. CUSTOMER VERIFICATION PROCESS
====================================================================

Customer verification ensures that every individual using the platform
is properly identified.

Verification consists of several independent stages.

Stage one verifies the customer's identity documents.

Stage two verifies ownership of the registered mobile phone number.

Stage three validates facial recognition using a live selfie.

Stage four evaluates address verification documents.

The following identification documents are accepted.

• National Identity Card
• Passport
• Government-issued Residence Permit

The following documents are not accepted.

• Expired identity cards
• Student cards
• Employee cards
• Temporary documents
• Photocopies

Identity documents are analyzed using automated document recognition
technology.

Optical character recognition extracts customer information from the
submitted documents.

Extracted information is compared with the registration information.

If inconsistencies are detected, the application is placed into manual
review.

Manual verification officers examine unclear or suspicious documents.

Customers receive status notifications whenever verification progresses
to a new stage.

Verification normally completes within forty-eight hours.

High application volumes may increase processing time.

Applications remaining in manual review longer than seventy-two hours
must be escalated to the Compliance Operations Team.

5. KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER (KYC)
====================================================================

Know Your Customer procedures are mandatory for every customer before
financial services become available.

The objective of KYC is to establish customer identity, reduce fraud
and satisfy regulatory obligations.

Customers are required to submit accurate information.

Providing false information constitutes a violation of company policy.

Required KYC documentation includes:

• Government-issued identification
• Live facial photograph
• Residential address verification
• Mobile phone verification
• Email verification

Additional documentation may be requested depending upon customer risk
classification.

Corporate customers must additionally provide:

• Business registration certificate
• Tax registration information
• Company ownership documents
• List of beneficial owners
• Board authorization documents

KYC information must remain accurate throughout the customer lifecycle.

Customers are responsible for notifying the company whenever personal
information changes.

Periodic KYC reviews are automatically scheduled every twenty-four
months.

Customers identified as high risk may require more frequent reviews.

Failure to complete requested KYC updates may result in temporary
account restrictions.

Repeated failure to provide updated information may result in account
closure.

6. CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE (CDD)
====================================================================

Customer Due Diligence is performed immediately after successful KYC
verification.

CDD evaluates customer behaviour, expected transaction volume and
overall financial risk.

Customer profiles are categorized into the following risk levels.

• Low Risk
• Medium Risk
• High Risk

Risk classification considers multiple factors including customer
occupation, country of residence, expected monthly transaction volume,
historical fraud indicators and regulatory watchlists.

Every customer receives an internal risk score.

Risk scores are continuously recalculated as new customer activity is
observed.

Changes in transaction behaviour may increase or decrease customer risk
classification.

Customers identified as low risk experience simplified monitoring.

Customers identified as medium risk receive standard transaction
monitoring.

Customers identified as high risk undergo enhanced monitoring and
additional compliance reviews.

CDD results remain confidential and are accessible only to authorized
employees.

====================================================================
7. ENHANCED DUE DILIGENCE (EDD)
====================================================================

Enhanced Due Diligence applies to customers presenting elevated
financial or regulatory risk.

EDD may be triggered by any of the following conditions.

Large international transfers.

Politically exposed persons.

Sanction list matches.

Unusual transaction behaviour.

High-value corporate customers.

Customers operating in high-risk industries.

EDD requires additional documentation including proof of income,
business ownership verification, source of funds documentation and
additional identification records.

Compliance officers may request supporting financial statements.

Enhanced Due Diligence investigations are documented using secure case
management software.

Every decision taken during an EDD investigation must include written
justification.

EDD cases remain open until every requested document has been reviewed.

Transactions associated with active EDD investigations may be delayed
until compliance review is completed.

8. ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING (AML)
====================================================================

The company maintains a comprehensive Anti-Money Laundering program to
prevent misuse of financial services for criminal activity.

Every financial transaction is evaluated using automated transaction
monitoring systems.

Transactions are assigned internal AML risk scores.

Factors considered include transaction frequency, transaction amount,
customer behaviour, geographic location, historical fraud indicators
and beneficiary information.

Large cash-equivalent transactions receive additional monitoring.

Transactions involving sanctioned jurisdictions are automatically
blocked.

Repeated transactions just below reporting thresholds are treated as
potential structuring attempts.

AML monitoring systems continuously compare customer activity against
historical behaviour patterns.

Suspicious activity generates alerts for manual compliance review.

Compliance analysts investigate every high-priority alert.

Investigations include examination of historical customer activity,
beneficiary information, device information and external regulatory
watchlists.

If suspicious behaviour is confirmed, the account may be temporarily
restricted while the investigation continues.

Compliance officers maintain complete audit records describing every
investigation decision.

Regulatory reporting requirements must be satisfied within legally
mandated reporting periods.

Employees must immediately report suspected money laundering activity
to the Compliance Department.

Failure to report suspicious activity may result in disciplinary action
including termination of employment.

====================================================================
9. TRANSACTION MONITORING
====================================================================

Transaction monitoring is one of the company's primary fraud prevention
and regulatory compliance mechanisms. Every financial transaction
processed by the platform is evaluated before, during and after
execution.

Monitoring is performed using automated rule engines, statistical
models and behavioural analysis systems. Every transaction contributes
to the customer's behavioural profile.

The monitoring platform evaluates multiple characteristics including:

• Transaction amount
• Transaction frequency
• Time of day
• Device fingerprint
• IP address
• Geographic location
• Historical customer behaviour
• Destination account
• Merchant category
• Previous fraud history

Customers whose behaviour significantly deviates from their historical
patterns may receive higher internal risk scores.

Multiple small transactions occurring within a short period may
indicate an attempt to bypass reporting thresholds.

Transactions performed immediately after password changes, device
changes or successful password recovery are considered higher risk.

Repeated transfers to newly added beneficiaries are monitored more
closely during the first thirty days.

High-value transfers originating from unfamiliar devices require
additional verification.

Transactions initiated from anonymous proxy networks, VPN services or
high-risk IP addresses receive additional scrutiny.

Every monitoring rule generates an internal confidence score.

The fraud detection platform combines multiple rule scores to calculate
an overall transaction risk score.

Transactions classified as low risk continue immediately.

Medium-risk transactions may require One-Time Password verification.

High-risk transactions may require manual compliance review before
completion.

Critical-risk transactions are automatically rejected.

Monitoring rules are reviewed monthly by the Risk Management Team.

Historical monitoring data is retained for audit purposes according to
regulatory retention requirements.

====================================================================

The company maintains an internal customer risk scoring system to
support fraud prevention, transaction monitoring and regulatory
compliance.

Every customer receives a continuously updated numerical risk score.

Risk scores are generated using multiple independent signals rather
than a single factor.

Examples of scoring inputs include:

Customer age of account.

Average transaction size.

Transaction velocity.

Number of failed login attempts.

Number of password reset requests.

Device consistency.

Country of residence.

Customer verification status.

Previous fraud investigations.

Chargeback history.

Dispute history.

Regulatory watchlist matches.

The scoring engine recalculates customer scores whenever significant
customer activity occurs.

Risk scores are categorized into the following levels.

Score 0-25
Very Low Risk

Score 26-50
Low Risk

Score 51-70
Medium Risk

Score 71-90
High Risk

Score 91-100
Critical Risk

Risk scores influence transaction approval workflows but never replace
human compliance decisions.

Compliance officers may manually override automated classifications
when sufficient supporting evidence exists.

Every manual override must be documented together with the reviewing
officer's justification.

Risk models undergo quarterly validation by the Data Science and Risk
Engineering teams.

11. PAYMENT PROCESSING POLICY
====================================================================

The payment processing platform is responsible for securely executing
financial transactions between customers, merchants and financial
institutions.

Every payment request follows a standardized processing pipeline.

Step 1
Authenticate customer.

Step 2
Validate session.

Step 3
Validate account status.

Step 4
Verify available balance.

Step 5
Execute fraud screening.

Step 6
Execute AML checks.

Step 7
Reserve customer funds.

Step 8
Execute payment.

Step 9
Generate audit records.

Step 10
Notify customer.

Every payment receives a globally unique payment identifier.

Payment identifiers are immutable and remain associated with the
transaction throughout its lifecycle.

Duplicate payment requests are detected using idempotency keys.

The payment gateway rejects duplicate payment identifiers.

Every successful payment generates an immutable audit trail.

Failed payments remain available for investigation but cannot be
modified.

Payment processing systems maintain high availability through redundant
application servers and replicated databases.

Payment processing services expose REST APIs secured using OAuth2 and
JWT authentication.

All communication between payment services is encrypted using TLS.

12. DOMESTIC MONEY TRANSFERS
====================================================================

Domestic transfers allow customers to transfer funds between supported
financial institutions within the country.

Transfers are available twenty-four hours per day subject to scheduled
maintenance windows.

The standard maximum amount permitted for a single domestic transfer is
USD 10,000.

Customers may perform a maximum of five domestic transfers per day.

The cumulative daily transfer limit is USD 25,000.

Transfers exceeding these limits require manual approval by the Risk
Operations Team.

Transfer requests exceeding customer account balances are rejected.

Beneficiary accounts must exist within supported financial institutions.

Recently added beneficiaries remain under enhanced monitoring for seven
days.

Transfers executed during fraud investigations may be delayed until
manual review is completed.

Customers receive push notifications immediately after successful
transfers.

Every completed transfer generates a digital receipt.

Digital receipts remain available for seven years.

Recipient full legal name.

Recipient financial institution.

Recipient country.

Recipient address.

Source of funds declaration.

Transfer confirmation notifications include reference numbers, exchange

Refund requests may only be submitted for eligible completed
transactions.

Customers must submit refund requests within thirty calendar days of

Refund eligibility depends upon merchant policies, transaction type and

Approved refunds are credited to the original funding source whenever

17. AUTHENTICATION AND AUTHORIZATION
====================================================================

Authentication is the process of verifying the identity of a customer,
employee or third-party application before granting access to company
resources.

Authorization determines which resources an authenticated identity is
permitted to access.

The company strictly separates authentication from authorization to
ensure consistent access control across all software systems.

Supported authentication methods include:

• Username and password
• One-Time Password (OTP)
• Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
• OAuth2
• OpenID Connect
• API Keys (Partner APIs only)
• JWT Access Tokens

Every authenticated session receives a unique session identifier.

Session identifiers are cryptographically random and cannot be
predicted.

Access permissions are granted according to the principle of least
privilege.

Users receive only the minimum permissions required to perform their
assigned responsibilities.

Administrative privileges are never granted permanently unless approved
through formal access management procedures.

Authorization decisions are enforced at every application layer,
including REST APIs, administrative portals and internal services.

Authorization failures are logged for audit and security analysis.

Repeated unauthorized access attempts automatically increase customer
risk scores.

18. PASSWORD POLICY
====================================================================

Passwords remain one of the most important authentication mechanisms
within the organization.

Customers must create passwords satisfying the following minimum
requirements.

Minimum length:
12 characters

Must contain:

• One uppercase letter
• One lowercase letter
• One numerical digit
• One special character

Passwords must not contain:

Customer name

Email address

Date of birth

Phone number

Dictionary words

Sequential keyboard patterns

Previously leaked passwords

Passwords expire every ninety days.

Customers receive reminder notifications beginning fourteen days before
password expiration.

The previous five passwords cannot be reused.

Passwords are never stored in plaintext.

All passwords are securely hashed using BCrypt before storage.

Password reset requests require successful identity verification.

Password reset links expire after fifteen minutes.

After five consecutive failed login attempts the customer account is
locked for thirty minutes.

Customers receive email and mobile notifications whenever passwords are
changed successfully.

====================================================================

Multi-Factor Authentication provides additional protection beyond
traditional passwords.

MFA combines at least two independent authentication factors.

Supported factors include:

Something the customer knows
(Password)

Something the customer has
(Mobile Device)

Something the customer is
(Biometric Authentication)

Customers must enable MFA before performing high-value financial
transactions.

The following activities require mandatory MFA verification.

International money transfers.

Adding a new beneficiary.

Changing account recovery information.

Updating registered mobile numbers.

Updating registered email addresses.

Accessing administrative portals.

MFA verification codes expire after five minutes.

Verification codes may only be used once.

Repeated invalid MFA submissions trigger temporary account lockout.

Lost devices require completion of identity verification before MFA can
be reset.

20. SESSION MANAGEMENT POLICY
====================================================================

Every authenticated user session is uniquely identified.

Session identifiers are generated using cryptographically secure random
number generators.

Sessions expire automatically after fifteen minutes of inactivity.

Absolute session lifetime is limited to eight hours regardless of user
activity.

Customers are automatically logged out after session expiration.

Session cookies are configured using the following security attributes.

Secure

HttpOnly

SameSite=Strict

Session identifiers are rotated immediately after successful login.

Session identifiers are also regenerated following password changes and
privilege escalation.

Multiple simultaneous sessions are permitted for customer accounts but
may be restricted for administrative users.

Administrative sessions are continuously monitored for suspicious
activity.

Sessions originating from different geographic regions within unusually
short periods generate fraud alerts.

Users may terminate active sessions through the account management
portal.

Terminated sessions become invalid immediately.

21. API SECURITY POLICY
====================================================================

All company APIs are designed according to secure-by-default
principles.

Every API endpoint requires authentication unless explicitly designated
as publicly accessible.

REST APIs use HTTPS exclusively.

Unencrypted HTTP requests are automatically redirected or rejected.

Partner APIs authenticate using OAuth2 access tokens.

JWT access tokens are digitally signed before issuance.

Token signatures are verified for every protected request.

Expired tokens are rejected immediately.

API rate limiting protects backend services from abuse.

Rate limits vary according to customer type and API subscription level.

Repeated rate-limit violations may result in temporary API suspension.

Input validation is performed for every API request.

Unexpected request parameters are rejected.

Request payloads are validated before business logic execution.

Sensitive information must never appear in URL query parameters.

API responses must never expose internal implementation details.

Detailed exception information is recorded internally while customers
receive standardized error messages.

25. INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY
====================================================================

The company maintains a formal Incident Response Program to ensure that
security incidents are identified, contained, investigated and resolved
in a timely and consistent manner.

A security incident is defined as any event that threatens the
confidentiality, integrity or availability of company systems,
applications, customer information or financial services.

Examples of security incidents include:

• Unauthorized account access
• Credential theft
• Malware infection
• Data leakage
• Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks
• Insider threats
• API abuse
• Ransomware attacks
• Database compromise
• Unauthorized privilege escalation

Every employee is responsible for reporting suspected security
incidents immediately.

Incident reports may originate from customers, automated monitoring
systems, employees, third-party security vendors or regulatory
authorities.

The Incident Response Team follows the following phases.

Phase 1 - Identification

Potential incidents are detected through monitoring systems, SIEM
alerts, application logs, fraud detection engines or customer reports.

Phase 2 - Validation

Security analysts determine whether the reported activity represents a
true security incident or a false positive.

Phase 3 - Containment

Affected systems may be isolated to prevent further damage.

Compromised credentials are revoked immediately.

Suspicious API keys are disabled.

Customer sessions may be terminated.

Phase 4 - Investigation

Security analysts identify the root cause, affected systems, attack
timeline and business impact.

Digital evidence is preserved throughout the investigation.

Phase 5 - Eradication

Malicious software, unauthorized accounts and compromised credentials
are removed.

Security patches are applied.

Configuration weaknesses are corrected.

Phase 6 - Recovery

Systems are restored gradually after verification.

Recovery activities include application validation, database integrity
checks and monitoring.

Phase 7 - Lessons Learned

Following every incident the organization conducts a post-incident
review.

Recommendations are documented.

Security improvements are prioritized.

Monitoring rules are updated where necessary.

Incident reports remain confidential and are retained according to
regulatory requirements.

Observability
The ability to understand system behaviour through logs, metrics and
distributed traces.

OTP
One-Time Password.

PII
Personally Identifiable Information.

RPO
Recovery Point Objective.

RTO
Recovery Time Objective.

Session
A temporary authenticated interaction between a user and the system.

TLS
Transport Layer Security.

Vector Database
A database optimized for storing and searching vector embeddings.

Embedding
A numerical representation of text used for semantic similarity
searches.

Chunk
A smaller section of a document generated during text splitting for
embedding and retrieval.

RAG
Retrieval-Augmented Generation, a technique where an AI model retrieves
relevant knowledge before generating an answer.
